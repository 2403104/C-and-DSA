A Miracle of Recovery: Overcoming Celiac Disease with Khatu Shyam's Blessings

In October 2023, I was diagnosed with celiac disease, just before my JEE (Joint Entrance Examination). This was a huge setback, as the symptoms of pain, fatigue, and digestive issues made it difficult to focus on my studies. It felt like my dreams of doing well in the exams were slipping away.

However, before August 2024, I had a profound experience. Lord Shiva appeared in my dream and reassured me that I would recover from this disease. His words gave me hope and comfort, despite the challenges I was facing.

In August 2024, I turned to Khatu Shyam Ji with complete faith, praying for healing and guidance. Remarkably, in just one month, my symptoms began to subside, and by 20th September 2024, I was able to reintroduce gluten into my diet without experiencing the discomfort I once had. It felt like a miracle, and I firmly believe that it was the blessings of Khatu Shyam Ji and Lord Shiva’s divine promise that brought about this recovery.

Despite the difficulties I faced with my health, I was able to qualify for both JEE MAINS and JEE ADVANCED 2024, something I had doubted was even possible while struggling with celiac disease. This victory is a testament to the power of faith, perseverance, and divine intervention.

Today, I am pursuing my Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) degree at IIT Goa. This journey—from overcoming illness to achieving my academic dreams—has been nothing short of miraculous. It has shown me that with unwavering faith and devotion, even the most difficult obstacles can be overcome.

I share this story to inspire others who might be facing their own struggles. When you have faith, the impossible becomes possible. Miracles happen, and with belief in the divine, we can conquer even the toughest challenges.
